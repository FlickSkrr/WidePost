Privacy Policy — WidePost

WidePost is a personal desktop program. It runs on your computer.

We do not operate a public website that collects visitor data. WidePost does not sell personal data.

What stays on your PC: video files you choose, YouTube/Instagram/TikTok tokens you paste, upload history.

What is sent to third parties: only when you click upload — the video and captions go to YouTube, Instagram or TikTok using their official APIs. Those companies then apply their own privacy policies.

Google/Meta/TikTok logins happen in their browsers. WidePost does not ask for those passwords.

Contact: the Gmail you use with this tool.
